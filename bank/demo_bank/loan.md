# Demo Bank loans

## Overview

Demo Bank is a prototype bank used for information only. This document explains the information a caller should check before applying for a loan.

The details below are illustrative demo values. Demo Bank offers personal, home, vehicle, business and gold loans.

## Documents required

Common documents are identity and address proof, the last three salary slips, six months of bank statements, income-tax returns and proof of the loan purpose.

Salaried applicants provide salary slips and an employment letter. Self-employed applicants provide business or income-tax records. Agricultural borrowers provide land or crop records where applicable.

## Steps

### Compare the loan options

1. Ask which loan purpose the caller has.
2. Check whether that loan type is in the Demo Bank documents.
3. Explain the eligibility and documents from the document that matches the purpose.
4. Tell the caller to compare the interest rate, processing fee, repayment period and total repayment cost.

Illustrative annual interest ranges from eight point five percent to eighteen percent, depending on the loan. The processing fee is one percent of the approved amount, and repayment periods range from one year to twenty years.

### Apply at a branch

1. Visit a Demo Bank branch.
2. Ask for the application checklist for the requested loan.
3. Complete the application and provide the requested documents.
4. Wait for Demo Bank's verification and decision.

Ask for the Demo Bank Loan Application. Branch applications are usually reviewed within five working days after all documents are received.

### Apply through the mobile app or website

Yes. Open the Demo Bank Mobile app or website, choose Loans, select the loan purpose, complete the application and upload the requested documents.

## Charges and limits

Illustrative annual rates are ten point five percent for a personal loan, eight point five percent for a home loan, nine point five percent for a vehicle loan, eleven percent for a business loan and nine percent for a gold loan.

The processing fee is one percent of the approved amount. Prepayment costs two percent of the outstanding principal. An illustrative late-payment charge is two percent of the overdue installment.

Loan amounts range from fifty thousand rupees to fifty lakh rupees, depending on the loan type and repayment capacity.

Applicants must be between twenty one and sixty years old and have an illustrative minimum monthly income of fifteen thousand rupees. Demo Bank also checks employment or business continuity and repayment capacity.

## Common mistakes

- Do not compare the interest rate without comparing fees and total repayment.
- Do not send identity documents through an unverified link.
- Do not pay a processing fee to an unverified person or account.
- Ask for every charge in writing before applying.

## FAQs

### What documents are needed for a loan?

Use the document list above. The branch gives the final checklist for the selected loan type.

### How long does approval take?

Most decisions take three to seven working days after complete documents are submitted.

### Can Demo Bank guarantee approval?

No. The bank must complete its checks. Demo Bank reviews eligibility, documents and repayment capacity, then communicates approval or the reason for rejection in writing.
