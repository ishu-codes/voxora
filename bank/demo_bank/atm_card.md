# Demo Bank ATM card activation and loss

## Overview

The details below are illustrative demo values. Demo Bank issues one debit card that can be used at ATMs and shops. The card must be activated before use.

## Documents required

The caller needs the card, the last four card digits and the mobile number registered with Demo Bank.

The full card number is never needed by this helper. Use the registered mobile number and a one-time verification shown inside the official app or website.

## Steps

### Activate at a Demo Bank ATM

1. Insert the card into a Demo Bank ATM.
2. Follow the on-screen activation option.
3. Set a new PIN when prompted.
4. Complete the on-screen confirmation.

At a Demo Bank ATM, insert the card and choose Card Services, then Activate Card. Set a four-digit PIN that is not a repeated or easy-to-guess number. Activation works at Demo Bank ATMs.

### Activate through the mobile app

1. Open the official Demo Bank mobile app.
2. Sign in without sharing credentials with anyone.
3. Open the card section.
4. Choose the activation option.
5. Complete the verification shown by Demo Bank.

Open the Demo Bank Mobile app, choose Cards, select the new card and choose Activate.

### Activate through the website

1. Open the official Demo Bank website.
2. Sign in to internet banking.
3. Open the card section.
4. Choose the activation option.
5. Complete the verification shown by Demo Bank.

Sign in to the official Demo Bank website, open Cards, select Activate Card and complete the displayed verification.

### Activate through the phone line

1. Call the official Demo Bank helpline.
2. Follow the card activation instructions.
3. Complete the verification required by Demo Bank.

Call the Demo Bank card helpline at one eight zero zero zero zero zero zero zero zero zero. The line is available from nine in the morning to six in the evening.

### Report a lost or stolen card

1. Block the card immediately using the official app, website or helpline.
2. Note the time and reference number of the blocking request.
3. Ask Demo Bank what replacement process and charges apply.
4. Follow up using an official Demo Bank channel.

Block the card through the Demo Bank Mobile app, website or helpline. The illustrative replacement fee is two hundred rupees, and the new card arrives in about five working days.

## Charges and limits

The illustrative card fee is one hundred rupees, the annual fee is one hundred fifty rupees, replacement costs two hundred rupees, and the ATM withdrawal limit is twenty five thousand rupees per day.

The first replacement card also costs two hundred rupees.

## Common mistakes

- Do not activate a card through a link received in a message.
- Do not share the ATM PIN, card PIN, OTP or full card number.
- Do not write the PIN on the card.
- Report a lost card as soon as possible.

## FAQs

### Can I activate the card at any bank ATM?

Activation is available at Demo Bank ATMs. Other banks' ATMs cannot activate a Demo Bank card.

### What should I do if activation fails?

Use an official Demo Bank channel and keep the error message or reference number. If the issue remains, ask the helpline to create a card-service complaint.

### Does blocking a card also stop automatic payments?

Yes. A blocked card cannot make ATM, shop or online card payments. Personalized payments may need to be set up again on the replacement card.
