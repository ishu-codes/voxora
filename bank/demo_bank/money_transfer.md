# Demo Bank money transfer

## Overview

Demo Bank provides information about sending money through digital and branch channels. UPI is an instant payment method. Net banking and the mobile app are internet-banking channels. A branch transfer is handled by bank staff.

## Steps

### Send money using UPI

1. Open the official Demo Bank mobile app or another UPI app connected to the account.
2. Choose the payment or send-money option.
3. Enter the recipient UPI ID or scan the recipient's QR code.
4. Check the recipient name and amount.
5. Enter the UPI PIN only inside the app.
6. Wait for the payment result and save the reference number.

### Send money using net banking

1. Sign in to the official Demo Bank internet-banking page.
2. Add the recipient and wait for any required beneficiary activation.
3. Choose the transfer type.
4. Enter the amount and check the recipient details.
5. Authorise the transfer with the method shown by Demo Bank.
6. Save the confirmation and reference number.

## Common mistakes

- Check the recipient name before approving a transfer.
- Never enter a UPI PIN to receive money.
- Never share a UPI PIN, OTP, password or card credentials.
- Save the reference number after every transfer.
- Use the official Demo Bank app or website.
