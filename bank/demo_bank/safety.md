# Demo Bank fraud safety

## Overview

Demo Bank never asks a customer to share a PIN, OTP, CVV, password, net-banking password or full card number on a call or through a message.

This bank helper has no access to an account and cannot check a balance, verify a transaction or perform a banking action.

## Steps

### Protect account information

1. Keep the PIN and password private.
2. Read an OTP only inside the official app or website where it was requested.
3. Never share an OTP with a caller, message sender or unofficial agent.
4. Do not install remote-access apps at a stranger's request.
5. End a suspicious call and use an official Demo Bank channel.

### Respond to a suspicious transaction

1. Block the card or access channel using an official Demo Bank channel if account access may be compromised.
2. Record the transaction date, amount and reference number.
3. Report the issue through the official Demo Bank channel.
4. Follow the complaint reference until Demo Bank closes it.

## Common mistakes

- A request to receive money never requires a UPI PIN.
- A genuine bank call does not require an OTP, PIN, CVV or password.
- A message can copy a bank name or logo and still be fraudulent.
- A request to install a screen-sharing app is a warning sign.
- A demand for an urgent payment to "unblock" an account is a warning sign.

## FAQs

### Does Demo Bank ask for an OTP on a call?

No. Never share an OTP with anyone.

### Can I share my full card number with a support caller?

No. This helper never asks for a full card number, PIN, CVV or password.

### Can this helper check my balance or stop a payment?

No. It provides information only. Use an official Demo Bank channel for account actions.
